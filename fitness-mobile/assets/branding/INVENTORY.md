## Documentation and reproducibility files

- `README.md`: findings, usage, approval issues, SDK guidance, next checkpoint.
- `INVENTORY.md`: this complete image inventory.
- `VALIDATION.md`: checks and limits.
- `brand-tokens.json`: seven authoritative colors, not imported into the app.
- `asset-manifest.json`: per-source/per-output dimensions, transparency, SHA-256 and edits.
- `source-checksums.json`: original hashes, including `.DS_Store`.
- `review/index.html`: local full gallery, source comparison, and 24/32pt navigation previews.
- `review/contact-somata.png`, `review/contact-fitadapt.png`, `review/contact-navigation.png`, `review/contact-app.png`: every processed PNG shown on Charcoal and Bone.
- `tools/prepare.cjs`: deterministic preparation and image assertions.
- `tools/contact-sheets.swift`: native macOS contact-sheet renderer.
- `tools/review-index.cjs`: inventory/gallery generator.
- `tools/validate.cjs`: read-only artifact/source validation.
