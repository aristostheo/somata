Premium Nutrition · offline review

Actual Nutrition JSX with synthetic fixture state. No real accounts, Firebase calls or device storage. Effects/subscriptions do not run. Browser gradients/SVG and the installed Ionicons font approximate native rendering; font scaling is simulated and safe insets are zero. The tab options use a static adapter; the edit sheet is not rendered. All actions are inert in these HTML files.

The pending fixture leaves saved targets at 2400 kcal. The past day omits today's targets. Loading, error and empty records remain distinct. Fixtures are only in tools/tests, never in the app.
